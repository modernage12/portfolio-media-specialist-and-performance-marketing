# Case 4 · A funnel built from scratch (2025-26)

**Client**: the same fitness and swimming center (anonymized), first season.
**Scope**: the whole acquisition function, set up alone in about two weeks.

## What I built

- **Landing page on Framer**: structure, copy, responsive layout, on-page SEO, Search Console
  indexing, domain connection.
- **Sign-up form** writing to a data sheet, plus **Calendly** for booking the visit: ad, page,
  form, booking, all in one flow.
- **Server-side tracking**: GTM, GA4, Stape and Meta Conversions API from day one.
- **Ad account from zero**: business verification (the cause of an early block turned out to be
  the missing verification, not the card) and payment setup.
- **Meta Ads**: test ad sets first, then scaling on the winning creative; creatives from stock
  images and Canva.
- **Google Ads Search**: granular structure (exact and phrase match, eCPC bidding). Limited by
  very low local search volume and then stopped: set up, not scaled.
- **Seasonal landing variants**: an offer version in season and a generic version out of season.

## Results (two flights, October 2025 and January 2026)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Unique leads during the flights | **57** |
| Spend | **~€950** |
| Blended cost per lead | **~€17** |
| Leads attributed by the Meta pixel | 35, at ~€27 each |
| CTR / CPC | ~1.83% / ~€0.17 |

## Honest limits

- Leads are form submissions (interest), not paying members: conversion to members is unknown.
- Per-channel attribution of the form was not tracked, so the 22 leads not attributed to Meta are
  not claimed by any channel.
- These limits are why the 2026 season (Cases 1 and 2) started with a stronger measurement setup.
